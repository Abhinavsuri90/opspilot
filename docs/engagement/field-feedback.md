# Field feedback template

## Observation
Describe the real workflow gap or operator behavior, with date and context.

## Evidence
Link anonymized examples, logs, or measurements; avoid customer data in this repository.

## Impact
Explain frequency, time or error cost, and who is affected.

## Proposed product change
State the smallest reusable change and alternatives considered.

## Decision and owner
Record outcome, owner, priority, and follow-up date.
