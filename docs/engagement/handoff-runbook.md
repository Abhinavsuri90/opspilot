# Customer handoff runbook template

## Ownership and contacts
Record operational owners, support contacts, and escalation paths.

## Deployment inventory
List service URLs, environments, config versions, dependencies, and secret owners without values.

## Daily operations
Describe review, approval, queue, and reporting routines.

## Failure response
Document alert triage, kill-switch use, retry decisions, rollback, and incident communication.

## Backup and restore
Record schedule, storage, retention, tested restore date, and responsible person.

## Access and offboarding
Document access requests, revocation, data export, and deletion steps.
