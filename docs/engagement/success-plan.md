# Success plan template

## Target outcomes
Define measurable accuracy, review, latency, and time-saved targets with owners.

## Baseline
Record how each current metric was measured and the source data.

## Evaluation plan
Describe the document sample, labels, acceptance rules, and dates for remeasurement.

## Timeline and milestones
List discovery, shadow mode, review, limited execution, and handoff checkpoints.

## Risks and mitigations
Record owner, trigger, impact, and response for each known risk.

## Sign-off
Record who approves policy changes and go-live.
